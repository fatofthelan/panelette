Panelette

Install from your browser

A Home Assistant touch panel for the Cheap Yellow Display — no IDE, no compiler.

Not sure? Leave it on the first one. If the screen comes up white, garbled, or with wrong colours, reconnect and pick another — reflashing takes a minute.

This browser can't flash over USB. Use Chrome, Edge, or Opera on a computer. Web Serial needs a secure connection — open this page over https.

Desktop Chrome / Edge / Opera only (Web Serial). On a phone? See "no computer?" below.

Before you start

  • A 2.8″ resistive-touch "CYD" (ESP32-2432S028R). Two verified boards: the ELEGOO 2-pack (USB-C, ILI9341) — the recommended one — and this ST7789 board. (Amazon affiliate links.)
  • A USB data cable that fits it. Many cheap cables are charge-only and won't work. On USB-C boards, use a USB-A–to–USB-C cable — many CYDs skip the resistors a C–to–C cable needs and won't power on with one.
  • A USB-serial driver if your computer doesn't see the board (CH340 on most, CP2102 on some): CH340 Windows · CH340 macOS (recent macOS has both built in).

What happens

  • Plug the board into USB and click Plug in & Install. Pick the serial port when asked.
  • The firmware flashes (about a minute). The board restarts on its own.
  • The installer asks for your Wi-Fi network and password and sends them to the panel.
  • Done. Click Visit Device to open the panel's settings page and add your tiles.
Board not detected, or the screen stays dark? On a USB-C board, use a USB-A–to–USB-C cable — many CYDs won't power on from a C–to–C one. If the flash won't start, hold the BOOT button while clicking Install and release it once it says "Connecting…".
Screen white, garbled, or wrong colours after flashing? The "ESP32-2432S028R" label covers several different display panels. Reconnect, pick a different display panel above, and install again — your config is kept. (ELEGOO and other USB-C boards are usually ILI9341; two-USB-port boards are often ST7789.)

No computer? (phone / Safari / Firefox)

Flash from a desktop as above, then skip the Wi-Fi step in the installer. The panel comes up as its own Wi-Fi network called PaneletteXXXX — join that from your phone and a setup page appears where you enter your network.

After setup

Open the panel's page (it prints its address on the Status screen), then connect it to Home Assistant with a long-lived access token. Full walkthrough: INSTRUCTIONS.md.
